How to use this guide. Read it with a pen and paper, but also play with the blue interactive diagrams — drag the vectors, move the sliders, watch the grid bend. Geometry is the heart of linear algebra: once you see a matrix warp space, a determinant become an area, or an eigenvector refuse to turn, the formulas stop being arbitrary. Every worked example is meant to be copied out by hand once, slowly. The orange highlights mark the thing to look at right now; green boxes are tips; red boxes are traps students fall into every year. Each section is its own page and remembers your light / dark choice. Good luck — you can do this.
